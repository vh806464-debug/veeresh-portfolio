External Cloud Media Hosting Architecture

Production Standard

When your website is published on Vercel or Netlify, serverless edge hosting is read-only. Media must be stored on dedicated cloud CDN storage rather than local web server files. Here is the recommended zero-cost setup:

YouTube / Shorts 100% Free

Limits: Infinite video uploads, 4K 60fps & HDR.
Cost: $0 forever. Best for motion reels, vertical Shorts, and client work. Zero bandwidth fees.

Cloudinary 25 GB Free/Mo

Limits: 25 Monthly Credits (~25 GB managed storage or 25 GB bandwidth).
Cost: $0 on Free Tier. Direct MP4/WebM streaming with auto-transcoding and thumbnail generation.

Supabase Storage 1 GB Free

Limits: 1 GB file storage, 2 GB monthly egress.
Cost: $0 on Free Tier. S3-compatible cloud storage with public HTTPS URLs.

ImgBB / PostImages Unlimited

Limits: Up to 32MB per image.
Cost: $0. Best for static design assets, styleframes, UI mockups, and poster thumbnails.
